EZ Soundboard starter pack
=======================

Sounds placed here ship inside the installer and are added to every new user's
library the first time they open EZ Soundboard (users can also re-add them from
"Add sounds" -> "Add starter sounds").

Layout:
  starter-sounds/
    Memes/       -> becomes the "Memes" board
    Callouts/    -> becomes the "Callouts" board
    Music/       -> becomes the "Music" board
    any-file.mp3 -> added without a board

Supported: mp3, wav, ogg, flac, m4a, aac, opus, webm.

IMPORTANT: only ship sounds you have the rights to distribute (your own recordings,
royalty-free / CC0 packs, or sounds you licensed). Meme clips cut from songs, movies,
TV or YouTube are usually copyrighted.
